# Calibration on 100 labelled prompts, 2026-09-25

`evals/calibration-100.jsonl`: 100 prompts across all six kinds (18 to 26 each), 45 trivial, 33 moderate
and 22 hard, 53 needing the workspace. 58 were typed inside a repository, including general questions
asked from inside one; 8 are in Dutch; three paste long logs, tracebacks or notes. Split 72 train /
28 test, stratified by kind. The 36 prompts of `evals/prompts.jsonl` serve as a second check.

## Labels

Two labellers answered the three questions independently: the author's first pass, and a separate
agent that saw only the prompts, the repository name and the router's question wording.

| Question | Agreement | Cohen's kappa |
|---|---|---|
| kind | 100% | 1.00 |
| difficulty | 90% | 0.84 |
| tools | 97% | 0.94 |
| resulting target | 91% | |

All 12 disagreements were ones the second labeller had marked as unsure. Each was settled against the
question's wording and recorded in the file (`labels.first`, `labels.second`, `adjudicated`): 8 went
to the second labeller (five moderate-to-hard, and three questions that name the current project and
so count as workspace), 4 to the first.

## Method

Raw router scores for all 136 prompts were computed once (semif with Qwen3.5-4B on MLX; Laya
typed-decisions for the hybrid's tools question). Temperatures were fitted per question on the 72
training prompts only (`ask calibrate --split train`), a single confidence floor was chosen on the
training prompts, and everything was then scored on the 28 held-out prompts and the old 36.

## Results

semif (the default), right target and fallback rate:

| | Train (72) | Test (28) | Old 36 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Previous defaults (raw scores, floors 0.45 / 0.45 / 0.55) | 77.8%, 8.3% fallback | 71.4%, 7.1% | 80.6%, 5.6% |
| No floor | 77.8%, 0% | 71.4%, 0% | 80.6%, 0% |
| Calibrated, no floor (shipped) | 77.8%, 0% | 71.4%, 0% | 80.6%, 0% |

Per question on the held-out 28: kind 89.3%, difficulty 82.1%, tools 78.6%.

Fitted temperatures (train): kind 0.955, difficulty 1.206, tools 2.159. Expected calibration error on
the held-out 28, raw to calibrated: kind 0.051 to 0.052, difficulty 0.149 to 0.131, tools 0.129 to
0.068. Temperatures never change which option wins; they make the percentages shown by `--ask` and
the dashboard mean what they say.

### Confidence floors

On the training prompts no floor from 0.3 to 0.8 routed more prompts right than no floor at all, so
the floor chosen on train was 0. Scored by direction over all 136 prompts:

| semif | Right | Too weak by 1 / 2 tiers | Too strong by 1 / 2 | Other vendor, same tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No floor | 105 | 13 / 5 | 9 / 3 | 1 |
| Previous defaults | 105 | 15 / 4 | 9 / 2 | 1 |
| Floor 0.55 | 101 | 14 / 4 | 9 / 2 | 6 |
| Floor 0.65 | 96 | 16 / 4 | 9 / 2 | 9 |

Falling back to Sonnet when unsure does not protect against sending work to a model that is too weak:
the too-weak count stays at 18 to 20 whatever the floor. The shipped default is therefore no floor.

### The hybrid

| | Train (72) | Test (28) | Old 36 | All 136, right |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| semif | 77.8% | 71.4% | 80.6% | 105 |
| Hybrid (Laya typed-decisions for tools) | 76.4% | 57.1% | 83.3% | 101 |

Laya answers the tools question better on average (test: 85.7% against semif's 78.6%) but fails on a
case the old 36 barely contained: general questions typed inside a repository. All four held-out
prompts where the two routers differ are of that kind ("what does ECONNREFUSED mean", "what does
`set -euo pipefail` do", ...): Laya scores "needs the workspace" at 0.61 to 0.72 and routes them to
Haiku, while semif says none at 0.85 to 1.00 and answers them locally. The earlier recommendation of
the hybrid, based on the 36 prompts, does not hold on this set.

## What this means

- Expect about 72 to 81% right targets on prompts like these; the 80.6% on the old 36 was measured on
  the set the question wording was tuned on.
- The remaining mistakes lean towards models that are too weak (18 of 136) rather than too strong (12).
  Difficulty is where most of them start (76 to 82% accurate), not the confidence thresholds.
- 28 held-out prompts is still small: one prompt is 3.6 points.
